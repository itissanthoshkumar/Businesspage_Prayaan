Skeleton — page specification

This is the Reflex Cover page with every value replaced by the field that fills it. A chip like {{ ph('business_name') }} marks a slot, in the position and at roughly the size the real value will occupy. Nothing here is read from the database, so this page looks the same whichever business you open it against.

Three fields are required — business_name, state and branch. Everything else is optional, and on a live page a section whose fields are all empty is not rendered at all: no heading, no empty card, no gap.

  • optionalphoto_url — a URL, not an upload. Used twice from the one file: the cover at 2:1 (21:8 above 900px) and a square-cropped avatar. With no photo the live page falls back to the centred hero instead of this one.
  • optionalcategory — the small caps line above the name. One short phrase, e.g. a trade.
  • requiredbusiness_name — the page's only h1. Also the browser title, the WhatsApp link-preview title and the footer disclaimer.
  • optionalowner_name — one line under the name. Also fills the "lends to owners like…" line in the enquiry card and the attribution under In numbers.
  • optionalestablished_year — renders as the Est. pill. Omitted, no pill.
  • optionaltier + tier_status — recognition badge, and both halves are needed: tier is gold, silver or bronze and picks the metal; tier_status is the qualifying line. A tier with no status never renders, by design — an unqualified badge on a lender's page invites the wrong reading.
  • optionalphones — a list; only the first two render, each its own 46px dial target.
  • optionallocality, district, state_name — joined with commas into one address line. Blanks are dropped, not left as stray commas.
  • optionalmap_url — becomes the "View on Google Maps" button. The URL itself is never shown on a live page.
