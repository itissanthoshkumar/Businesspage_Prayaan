What you get

A free page that can pay you back.

Search for most small businesses and there is nothing — no photograph, no timings, no number that dials. The page fixes that, and costs you nothing.

Then it keeps working. Anyone who opens your link and needs a loan can leave their number on it. If their loan is disbursed, you earn a referral reward — you made the introduction. They get the loan they came for, on the terms they were quoted.

Read the referral terms — what is earned, when, and what it is not.

How the referral works

Five steps, and only the last one pays.

{# Illustrations are authored inline SVG, stroked in currentColor so each one takes its own stage colour. No image request, nothing external. #}
  1. 1 You share your page One tap opens WhatsApp. Family, your groups, your regular customers. You earn
  2. 2 Someone needs a loan They open your link and leave a name and a number. Nothing more.
  3. 3 Prayaan calls them A person from the branch, not a form. The enquiry arrives credited to your page.
  4. 4 The loan is assessed On its own merits. Your referral does not change the decision or the terms.
  5. 5 It is disbursed They get their loan. Only now is your referral reward earned.

No reward is earned on an enquiry, on an application, or on a sanction — only on disbursal. It is never a fee you pay, and referring someone does not make you an agent of Prayaan Capital. Full terms.

Your page

Your page holds four things.

The shop

One photograph, its EXIF and GPS stripped before it is ever stored.

The trade

What you sell, the years you have been open, the hours you keep.

The number

Up to two mobile numbers, one tap to dial. No form to reach you.

The enquiry

A name and a number from anyone who needs a loan. That is the referral.

Standing

What the Gold, Silver and Bronze badges mean.

A page may carry a standing. It reflects referrals you have made and whether you are active in the programme, and it appears on the page itself.

It is not a rating of your business, and it says nothing about anyone's borrowing or repayment — yours or theirs.

The rules

What this deliberately never does.

  • It is never a fee you pay. Prayaan never asks for money before loan processing, and no reward scheme changes that.
  • It never changes a decision. A referral does not affect whether a loan is approved, or on what terms. Every application is assessed on its own merits.
  • It does not make you an agent. Referring someone does not make you an agent of Prayaan Capital, and you must not represent yourself as one.
  • Nothing publishes without your consent. The branch records how you agreed before your page can go live.
  • Your page is never listed. No directory, here or in search of this site. Your own link is the only way in, and it never shows your street address or anything about your borrowing.

Why Prayaan built it: to meet borrowers through the people who already know them, instead of through bought lists and cold calls. Referral terms · Grievance & GRO.
