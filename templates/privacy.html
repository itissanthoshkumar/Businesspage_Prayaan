This notice covers two different sets of people: the business owners whose pages are published here, and the visitors who fill in the enquiry form.

If you filled in the enquiry form

We collect your name, mobile number and, if you gave it, your pincode. We also record which business page you came from, and a one-way hash of your IP address to stop the form being abused. The raw IP is never stored.

We use it for one thing: to call you back about a loan enquiry, and to pass you to the Prayaan branch nearest to you if you want to go further. We do not sell it and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

TO CONFIRM before launch. How long enquiry records are kept before they are deleted, and who owns that deletion. This is open question OQ5 and has not been decided.

If a page here is about your business

The page shows what was collected for it: business name, owner name, category, locality and district, a photograph, a description, opening hours, languages and up to two phone numbers. We do not publish your street address — the location shown is the locality, and any map link points at the business.

Nothing on the page states or implies anything about any loan, and we do not publish loan amounts, dates or repayment information.

You can have the page corrected or removed at any time, without giving a reason — see report or remove a page.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act you may ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, correction of it, or its erasure, and you may withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent for the page takes the page down.
