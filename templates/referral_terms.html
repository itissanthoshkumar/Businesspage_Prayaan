Business pages carry a line saying that if a loan is disbursed, the business whose page introduced the borrower earns a referral reward. These are the terms behind that line.

{% if referral_rate_pct and referral_rate_pct > 0 %} {% set rate_s = '%g'|format(referral_rate_pct) %}

What the reward is

{{ rate_s }}% of the disbursed loan amount, paid to the business whose page introduced the borrower. On a loan of ₹10,00,000 that is ₹{{ (1000000 * referral_rate_pct / 100)|inr }}.

The percentage applies to the amount actually disbursed, not to the amount enquired about, applied for or sanctioned. The items under Still to be confirmed below are not settled, and the rate alone is not the whole offer.

{% endif %}

When a reward is earned

  • Only when a loan is disbursed. Not on enquiry, not on application, not on sanction.
  • The enquiry must have come through the referring business's own page.
  • Only the referring business earns. The person who takes the loan does not receive a referral reward — their benefit is the loan itself, on the terms they were quoted.

What it is not

  • It is not a fee you pay. Prayaan never asks for money before loan processing, and no reward scheme changes that.
  • It does not affect whether a loan is approved, or on what terms. Every application is assessed on its own merits.
  • Referring someone does not make you an agent of Prayaan Capital, and you must not represent yourself as one.

Recognition tiers

A business page may show a Gold, Silver or Bronze standing. That reflects referrals made and whether the customer is active in the programme. It is not a rating of the business, and it says nothing about anyone's borrowing or repayment.
