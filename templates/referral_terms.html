Business pages carry a line saying that if a loan is disbursed, both you and the business that referred you earn a referral reward. These are the terms behind that line.

When a reward is earned

  • Only when a loan is disbursed. Not on enquiry, not on application, not on sanction.
  • The enquiry must have come through the referring business's own page.
  • Both sides are eligible: the person who took the loan, and the business whose page introduced them.

What it is not

  • It is not a fee you pay. Prayaan never asks for money before loan processing, and no reward scheme changes that.
  • It does not affect whether a loan is approved, or on what terms. Every application is assessed on its own merits.
  • Referring someone does not make you an agent of Prayaan Capital, and you must not represent yourself as one.

Recognition tiers

A business page may show a Gold, Silver or Bronze standing. That reflects referrals made and whether the customer is active in the programme. It is not a rating of the business, and it says nothing about anyone's borrowing or repayment.
