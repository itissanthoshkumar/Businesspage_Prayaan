If a page carries your business and you want it taken down, we will remove it. You do not have to give a reason, and you do not have to be the person who asked for it.

What happens

  1. Tell us which page, using the link or the business name.
  2. We take the page offline. The address then behaves exactly like an address that never existed.
  3. We ask search engines to drop the cached copy. That part is not instant and is not entirely in our hands.

Other things you can ask for

  • A correction — a wrong phone number, a wrong locality, an out-of-date photo.
  • Removal of one detail rather than the whole page.
  • A copy of what we hold about you, or deletion of it. See the privacy notice.
