{# Recognition tier — Gold / Silver / Bronze. THE RULE, enforced here and not negotiable in a template: a badge renders only when `tier_reason` is present. A bare "Gold" tells a visitor this business was ranked, without saying against what — and the obvious guess, for a page published by a lender, is that it reflects how well they repay. That would be publishing credit information about a named person, and it would expose the one thing this page is careful never to state: that the customer borrows from Prayaan at all. THE BASIS IS REFERRALS AND ACTIVITY — how many people this customer introduced to Prayaan, and whether they are still active in the programme. Nothing else. Not years in business, not shops served, not turnover: those are the business's own numbers and belong in the "In numbers" panel, not in a Prayaan-awarded tier. And explicitly never repayment history, bureau scores, exposure or internal risk grades — publishing any of those would disclose credit information about a named person. The COUNT is not published — "31 referrals" told a stranger how much business this customer sends a lender, which is nobody else's business. What shows is the standing only: Gold / Silver / Bronze and whether they are Active. The count stays internal and drives the tier. A status string is still REQUIRED. A badge reading only "Gold", with nothing qualifying it, is the case this guard exists to prevent. Expects: page.tier in (gold|silver|bronze) and page.tier_status. #} {% if page.tier in ('gold', 'silver', 'bronze') and page.tier_status %} {{ page.tier|capitalize }} {{ page.tier_status }} {# Three glint stars, staggered — pure CSS twinkle (Magic UI's sparkles effect ported without its JavaScript; the page ships script-src 'none'). #} {% endif %}
